# CIVIS – Civilization Intelligence System

AI-Driven Decision Laboratory for Psychological, Economic & System-Level Simulation

---

## 1. Overview

CIVIS (Civilization Intelligence System) is a cloud-native AI simulation platform designed to model complex behavioral, financial, and economic systems before real-world implementation.

Unlike traditional analytics tools that evaluate isolated financial or economic metrics, CIVIS integrates:

- Psychological behavior modeling
- Multi-agent economic simulation
- Incentive redesign experimentation
- Collapse risk forecasting
- Systemic stability measurement

CIVIS enables proactive decision-making by simulating system outcomes digitally before deployment in reality.

## 2. Core Capabilities

### Behavioral Intelligence
- Cognitive bias detection
- Risk tolerance mapping

- Emotional stability modeling

### Financial Simulation
- Monte Carlo projections
- Career vs startup modeling
- Cash runway forecasting
- Burnout probability estimation

### Multi-Agent Simulation
- Configurable agent populations

- Cooperation vs competition weighting

### Incentive Redesign Lab
- UBI simulation
- Reputation-based economy modeling
- Contribution-weighted reward systems
- Custom policy parameter tuning
